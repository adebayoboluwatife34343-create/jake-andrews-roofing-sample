FAQ

Common questions

How much does a new roof or re-roof cost?

Every roof is different, depending on size, pitch, access and materials, so Jake quotes per job. Quotes are free.

Do you fix leaks and small repairs?

Yes, roof repairs are one of the core services. Call or send a photo and a short description to get started.

What is ClearLight roofing?

Clear roofing panels used on decks, pergolas, carports and lean-tos to keep the rain off while letting light through.

Which areas do you cover?

Auckland's North Shore, based in Bayview. Ask about jobs further afield.

Do I need building consent for a re-roof?

It depends on the job. Replacing a roof with similar materials is often exempt, but check with Auckland Council or ask Jake when he quotes.
